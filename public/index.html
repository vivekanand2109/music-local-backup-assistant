Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about downloading lossless audio with Appllify Music.

How do I download music in Lossless ALAC or Hi-Res 24-bit? ▾

Paste any Apple Music, Spotify, YouTube, or SoundCloud link into the top search bar. In the Audio Quality pill, choose Hi-Res Lossless (up to 24-bit/192kHz) or Lossless (ALAC 24-bit/48kHz). Click Fetch & Download, and your tracks will download bit-for-bit in studio master quality with original album art and synced lyrics embedded.

What is the difference between ALAC and FLAC? ▾

ALAC (Apple Lossless Audio Codec) is Apple's official lossless codec stored in standard .m4a files. It plays natively on macOS Music.app, iOS devices, Finder, and Safari without extra software. FLAC is the open-source audiophile standard. Both preserve 100% of the original studio recording without any quality loss.

Can I download full albums or playlists in one click? ▾

Yes! Appllify Music fully supports complete album and playlist downloads. When you paste an album link, the complete tracklist loads instantly with individual song sizes. Clicking Download Album (1-Click) saves all songs into clean, organized artist and album folders.

How do I use Appllify Music on my iPhone or Android device? ▾

Simply open https://appllifymusic.netlify.app on your phone. In Safari on iOS, tap Share → Add to Home Screen. On Android Chrome, tap the menu and select Install App. Appllify functions as a full-screen, native-feeling music app on mobile with touch controls and background streaming.

Where are downloaded music and video files saved? ▾

Downloaded media is saved directly into your device's ~/Downloads/Apple Music/ folder organized by Artist Name/Album Title/. You can click Show in Finder at any time to open the folder directly.
